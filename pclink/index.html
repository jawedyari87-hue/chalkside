Chalkside PC link

Shows your tutor what's on this PC's screen, such as your lecture slides, alongside your tablet. It also shows you the tutor's board here, so your tablet stays free for writing. The PC and tablet must be on the same Wi-Fi, with a lesson running in the Chalkside app.

First, put this page on your PC. Browsers don't let a website reach your tablet over Wi-Fi, so the PC link runs as a file on your PC.

Click Download, then open the file it saves. It opens in your browser, ready to use. Keep it somewhere handy, such as your desktop, for next time.

Both are on the Chalkside app's start screen, under "Your PC's screen".

Choose the window with your slides rather than the whole screen, so the tutor doesn't see its own board. Keep this tab open while you study (it can be in the background). The tutor gets a new picture only when something changes.

The board is in its own window, on top of your other windows. Move it with its title bar, and drag its edges to resize it.
