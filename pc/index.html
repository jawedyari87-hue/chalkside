Chalkside PC

Study maths, coding and computer science with a voice tutor that can see the screen or window you share: lecture slides, a PDF, your code, a terminal. Talk to it, and it writes the maths and code in its notes.

This part of Chalkside is for a computer. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on your PC.

1. Share a screen or window

A single window, such as your code editor or the slides, works best: the tutor then never sees its own notes. You can share a second one as well, such as your slides and your code, or both of your monitors.

2. Open the tutor

Open the tutor puts it in a small window that stays on top of your other apps (drag its edges to resize it; browsers don't let it grow beyond a certain size). Open it in a big window gives it an ordinary window instead, which you can maximise or make full screen, on a second monitor say, but which doesn't stay on top. Press Start tutor there, and allow the microphone when asked.

Good to know

  • Keep this page open while you study. You can minimise it; closing it closes the tutor.
  • The tutor gets a new picture only when your screen changes. The eye button in its window hides your screen from it. With two shared, each picture says Screen 1 or Screen 2 across the top, so the tutor can tell them apart.
  • It needs your free Gemini key: the first time, the tutor window asks for it. Its settings are in the sliders button.
  • To open Chalkside PC like an app, click Install in Chrome's or Edge's address bar.
